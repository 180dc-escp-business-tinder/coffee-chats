Business Tinder privacy

Business Tinder is 180DC ESCP's internal tool for cross-campus coffee chats, run by the People & Organisation team. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Who can use it

Only 180DC members with an @180dc.org email address. Anyone else is refused at sign-in.

What we collect

  • Your 180DC email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. We don't receive your password or anything else from your Google account.
  • What you enter in your profile: codename, real name, contact email, campus, team, role, skills, fun fact and how often you want a chat.
  • Who you pick in the deck, your matches, and your feedback after each chat.

Who sees what

  • All members: your blind card, meaning codename, campus, skills and fun fact.
  • Your matches: your real name, email, team and role, once a round is sent.
  • P&O admins: everything above, plus your picks and feedback, to run the rounds.

Nothing is sold, shared outside 180DC ESCP, or used for advertising.

Where it's stored

The data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and the site is hosted on Netlify. Sign-in emails are sent from the Business Tinder Gmail account.

Deleting your data

Ask P&O, or email businesstinder.180dc.escp@gmail.com, and we'll remove your profile and history.
